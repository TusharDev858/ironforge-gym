Who We Are

MORE THAN JUST A GYM

Founded in 2012, IronForge was built on a simple belief: every person has the potential for extraordinary strength. We created a space where beginners and elite athletes alike can train hard, grow fast, and forge the body and mindset of a champion.

Over 12 years, we've helped thousands of members lose weight, build muscle, run their first 5K, lift their first barbell, and most importantly — discover what they're truly capable of.
